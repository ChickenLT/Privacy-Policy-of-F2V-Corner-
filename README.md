# Privacy-Policy-of-F2V-Corner-

F2V Corner Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the F2V Corner Bot Privacy Policy. This policy explains how our Discord bot may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use its moderation and music features.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary for the Bot to function properly.

By using the F2V Corner Bot, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled and how the Bot is configured, we may process the following information:

* Discord user IDs: To identify users who interact with the Bot.
* Discord server IDs: To identify servers where the Bot operates.
* Moderation records: Information about warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, or other moderation actions, if these features are enabled.
* Command information: Commands and settings needed to respond to requests.
* Music information: Song titles, search queries, playback settings, and queue information needed to provide music features.
* Technical logs: Error reports and diagnostic information used to maintain and improve the Bot.

We do not intend to collect sensitive personal information unless it is strictly necessary and lawfully permitted.

Only include the categories above that your Bot actually processes.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

* Provide moderation and music functionality.
* Execute commands requested by users with appropriate permissions.
* Maintain moderation records where configured.
* Remember server preferences and Bot settings.
* Diagnose technical problems and prevent abuse.
* Improve the reliability and security of the Bot.

We do not sell users’ personal information.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in a database, configuration file, or log system, depending on how the Bot is hosted.

Data should only be retained for as long as necessary for its stated purpose. Moderation records and server settings may be retained until they are deleted, become unnecessary, or the Bot is removed from the server, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Actual retention periods depend on the Bot’s configuration.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared when necessary to:

* Operate the Bot and its hosting infrastructure.
* Provide third-party music or technical services used by the Bot.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.
* Protect the Bot, its users, or the community against abuse or security threats.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Discord and Third-Party Services

The Bot operates on Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord’s Privacy Policy and Discord’s Terms of Service.

Music playback may depend on external services. The Bot does not control the privacy practices of third-party providers.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Bot against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Rights and Data Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the Bot.

To submit a request, contact the Bot owner or the F2V Corner server staff through the official Discord server.

We may need to verify your request before taking action. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord’s minimum age requirements and applicable laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same public link, with the updated date shown at the top.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a data request, contact the Bot owner or the F2V Corner server staff through the official Discord server.

⸻

By using the F2V Corner Bot, you acknowledge that you have been informed about the way information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.
